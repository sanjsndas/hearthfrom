    Cookies Policy

    How we use cookies and similar technologies
    Last updated: 15 January 2025

    Hearth Form Theory ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on hearthformtheory.com to improve your browsing experience and help the site function properly. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, which ones we use, and how you can control them.

    We keep cookies to a minimum. We do not use advertising cookies, we do not track you across other websites, and we do not sell any data collected through cookies. Most cookies we use are essential for the site to work.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the site owners. Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser).

    Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels. When we refer to "cookies" in this policy, we mean all of these technologies.

    2. How We Use Cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    • Essential functionality: To ensure the website loads correctly, remembers your cookie consent choice, and keeps basic security features working.
    • Preferences: To remember settings you've chosen, such as whether you've dismissed a notification or your preferred reading view.
    • Analytics: To understand in aggregate how readers use our site — which articles are popular, how visitors navigate, and where we can improve. We use a privacy-respecting, cookie-less analytics approach that does not track individuals across sites.

    3. Types of Cookies We Use

    The table below describes the specific cookies and similar technologies we use on Hearth Form Theory:

    Name Type Purpose Duration
    hft_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent preference so we don't ask again on every visit. 12 months
    hft_session Essential Maintains basic site functionality and security during your visit. Session
    hft_prefs Preference Remembers minor UI preferences (e.g., search bar state, dismissed banners). 6 months
    Analytics (cookie-less) Analytics Aggregated, anonymous page-view statistics. No personal identifiers, no cross-site tracking. N/A
    Newsletter platform Third-party Set only if you interact with our newsletter signup form. Used to manage subscriptions and prevent spam. Up to 12 months

    Essential cookies

    These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as setting your privacy preferences or filling in forms. You can set your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, but some parts of the site may not work as a result.

    Preference cookies

    These cookies allow the site to remember choices you make (such as your language or the region you are in) and provide enhanced, more personal features. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages. If you do not allow these cookies, some of these features may not function properly.

    Analytics

    We use a privacy-first analytics approach that does not rely on cookies and does not collect personal data. It records aggregated information such as page views, referral sources, and general geographic region (country level). This helps us understand which articles resonate with readers without compromising anyone's privacy.

    Third-party cookies

    Our newsletter signup form may set cookies from our email service provider when you interact with it. These are used solely to manage your subscription and prevent abuse. We do not use social media cookies, advertising cookies, or any cross-site tracking technologies.

    4. Your Cookie Choices

    You have several options for controlling or deleting cookies:

    • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse or accept cookies, delete specific cookies, or set preferences for certain websites. Check your browser's help section for instructions (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
    • Opt-out of analytics: Because our analytics are cookie-less and anonymized, there is no individual tracking to opt out of. You can still use browser privacy tools if you prefer.
    • Do Not Track: Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. We honor this signal by disabling non-essential analytics when it is present.
    • Consent banner: If you have previously accepted cookies, you can clear your browser cookies to reset your preference, or contact us to update your choice.

    Blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of this and many other websites you visit. If you disable cookies, some features of Hearth Form Theory may not work as intended.

    5. Cookies We Do Not Use

    For clarity, we do not use:

    • Advertising or targeting cookies
    • Social media tracking pixels (e.g., Facebook Pixel, Twitter/X Pixel)
    • Cross-site tracking cookies from data brokers
    • Cookies that build personal profiles for marketing purposes

    We believe in a calm, respectful reading experience — not surveillance.

    6. Third-Party Services

    We work with a small number of trusted service providers who may set cookies as part of their service:

    • Email newsletter platform: Used to deliver our newsletter and manage subscriptions. Only relevant if you interact with the signup form.
    • Website hosting provider: May set essential cookies for security and load balancing.

    We do not allow third parties to use cookies on our site for their own marketing or tracking purposes.

    7. Updates to This Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
