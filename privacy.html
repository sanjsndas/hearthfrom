    Privacy Policy

    How we handle your information
    Last updated: 15 January 2025

    Hearth Form Theory ("we", "us", "our") operates the website hearthformtheory.com and is committed to protecting the privacy of our readers. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to all visitors, subscribers, and contributors to our design journal.

    We keep things simple: we collect only what we need to run the journal, we never sell your data, and we use minimal third-party services that respect your privacy. You can opt out of our newsletter at any time with a single click.

    1. Information We Collect

    We collect information in the following ways:

    Information you provide directly

    • Newsletter subscription: When you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address. Optionally, you may provide your first name.
    • Contact form: When you contact us via our contact page, we collect your name, email address, subject, and the message you send.
    • Comments and contributions: If you submit a comment, pitch, or correction, we collect the content of your message and any contact details you choose to share.

    Information collected automatically

    Like most websites, we automatically receive certain technical information when you visit:

    • Log data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and the date/time of your visit.
    • Device information: Screen size, device type (desktop, mobile, tablet), and general location (country/city level, derived from IP).
    • Analytics: We use a privacy-respecting, cookie-less analytics approach that aggregates data without tracking individual users across sites. No personal profiles are built.

    Cookies and similar technologies

    We use a minimal set of cookies and local storage to remember your preferences (such as whether you closed a banner) and to keep the site functioning. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies. For full details, please see our Cookies Policy.

    2. How We Use Your Information

    We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

    • To deliver our newsletter: If you subscribe, we use your email address to send you weekly or bi-weekly editorial updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.
    • To respond to your inquiries: If you contact us, we use your details to reply to your message and handle any follow-up.
    • To improve our content and site: Aggregated analytics help us understand which articles are useful and how readers navigate the site, so we can make better editorial decisions.
    • To maintain security and prevent abuse: We may use technical data to detect and prevent spam, fraud, or other malicious activity.
    • To comply with legal obligations: In rare cases, we may need to retain or disclose information to meet applicable laws or respond to lawful requests.

    3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

    If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for processing your personal data is:

    • Consent: For sending our newsletter and for any non-essential cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.
    • Legitimate interests: For replying to your messages, improving our site, and ensuring security. We balance these interests against your rights.
    • Legal obligation: Where we are required to retain data for legal or regulatory reasons.

    4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

    We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share information only with the following trusted service providers, and only to the extent necessary:

    • Email service provider: We use a reputable newsletter platform to send emails and manage subscriptions. Your email address is stored on their servers under a data processing agreement.
    • Hosting provider: Our website is hosted on secure servers. The hosting provider may process technical log data as part of normal operations.
    • Legal authorities: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our team or readers.

    5. International Data Transfers

    Our service providers may store or process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where we transfer personal data from the EEA to other countries, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

    6. Data Retention

    We keep your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above:

    Data type Retention period
    Newsletter subscription (email, name) Until you unsubscribe, plus 30 days for suppression list
    Contact form messages 2 years from last correspondence
    Server log data 30–90 days (rotated automatically)
    Analytics aggregates (non-personal) Indefinitely (no personal identifiers)

    7. Your Rights

    Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

    • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
    • Rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
    • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (the "right to be forgotten").
    • Restriction: Ask us to limit how we process your data.
    • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
    • Data portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
    • Withdraw consent: Unsubscribe from our newsletter or withdraw cookie consent at any time.

    To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@hearthformtheory.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

    8. Security

    We take the security of your data seriously. Our website uses HTTPS encryption, we implement access controls, and we work only with reputable service providers who maintain high security standards. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    9. Children's Privacy

    Hearth Form Theory is a design journal intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

    10. Third-Party Links

    Our articles may contain links to external websites, including those of designers, architects, or product makers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing any personal data.

    11. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will post a notice on our website and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

    12. Contact Us

    If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact our data protection contact:

    Hearth Form Theory
    Attn: Privacy Inquiries
    Via Brera 12, 20121 Milano, Italy
    Email: privacy@hearthformtheory.com

    This policy is written in plain language because we believe privacy shouldn't require a law degree to understand. If anything is unclear, just ask — we're happy to explain.
