    Frequently asked questions

    What's the first step in planning a renovation?

    Start with a clear brief: how you want to live in the space, not just how it should look. Measure everything, prioritise natural light, and define a realistic budget before engaging designers or builders.

    How do I choose the right lighting for a room?

    Think in layers: ambient (general), task (focused), and accent (decorative). Use dimmers wherever possible, and match colour temperature to the function — warmer for relaxation, cooler for work.

    Is sustainable design more expensive?

    Not necessarily. Passive design, thoughtful orientation, and durable materials often cost less over time. Many sustainable choices — like eliminating unnecessary square footage — actually reduce build cost.

    How do I make a small room feel bigger?

    Maximise natural light, use consistent flooring, choose furniture with legs (to show floor), and avoid heavy curtains. Mirrors and lighter tones help, but good space planning matters most.

    What's the difference between interior design and interior architecture?

    Interior architecture involves structural changes — moving walls, altering layouts, and working with building systems. Interior design focuses on finishes, furniture, lighting and the sensory experience within that shell.
